Bones are the part of BARF people mess up most often. Me included. Too many and your dog's stools are hard as concrete, too few and it's missing calcium long-term. Good news: your dog will show you when something's wrong.

## How to tell the ratio is off

- **White, crumbly stools** — too many bones. Cut back.
- **Soft, loose stools** — can mean there aren't enough.
- **Off the ratio long-term** — risky for bone development in puppies, joints in adult dogs.

## What I do about it

In the app, every ingredient has its bone share, so I can see how much of the daily limit my dog has already eaten. When I'm close to the cap, the Coach suggests what to add instead of bones. No math at the fridge.

Other than that, one simple rule: watch the stools. If something changes, adjust the ratio by a few percent and keep watching. If that doesn't help, see a vet.
