I built my own chatbot into the app. It's called the Coach, and it differs from general AI in one thing: it knows what's in your fridge. Because you track it in the app.

## What it can do

You type "what should I feed today?" and the Coach puts together a portion from what you actually have at home. It watches the daily limits while doing it — bones, organs and muscle meat. If your dog has had enough bones for the day, the Coach simply won't put them in the suggestion and reaches for something else.

You confirm the suggestion with one tap and it goes straight into the feeding diary. Or you dismiss it and ask for another one. It works the other way too — you can ask "am I low on calcium?" and the Coach checks the macro overview.

## What not to ask of it

The Coach is not a vet. If your dog has a health problem, a special diet, or you're completely new to raw feeding, talk to someone who does this professionally first. The Coach is a helper for the everyday "what goes in the bowl" — not an authority on your dog's health.

## What it costs

Running AI costs money — every message goes through an API. That's why the Coach has a daily message limit. The app is in test mode for now and everything is free. We'll see.
